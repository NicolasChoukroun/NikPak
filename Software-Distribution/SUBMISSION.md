## One section per site

### 1. Softpedia

Submit at https://www.softpedia.com/user/submit.html. PAD file URL preferred (automatic version tracking); two direct HTTP/FTP download links, 32x32 icon, a screenshot and a buy link. Review 24-48 h. Links are nofollow.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

Category to pick: **Software > System / File Managers / Compression**.

> Their 2026 policy rejects software whose code was generated by AI assistants. Read it before submitting.

### 2. Download3K

Submit at https://www.download3k.com/submit.php. PAD only. No version number in the PAD URL, never change the URL, update the same file; they re-check it daily. Separate PAD per edition; OS in the program name when the builds differ.

The PAD file is the only accepted format — nothing else gets in.

Category to pick: **System Utilities -> File Compression**.

> Explicitly rejects adult material and anything whose download link is not permanently available.

### 3. GearDownload

Submit at https://www.geardownload.com/submit.php. PAD only. You pick their category first, then paste the PAD URL. 246,000 PAD records, ~54,000 approved. File upload is disabled, the PAD URL is the only route.

The PAD file is the only accepted format — nothing else gets in.

Category to pick: **System Utilities -> File Compression**.

> Different products sharing one download, screenshot URL or order system are flagged as spam and blacklisted.

### 4. Sooftware

Submit at https://www.sooftware.com/submit. Free developer account, then PAD file or manual form. Windows, macOS and Android. You must own all rights in what you submit.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.
 Create the developer account first.

> They state they receive many submissions and ask for patience.

### 5. FilePuma

Submit at https://www.filepuma.com/submit_program/. Dedicated 'if you have a PAD file, submit it here' link, or the manual form. Every submission is tested and scanned with VirusTotal and Google Safe Browsing.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

> Names, icons and screenshots are taken as licensed to them on submission; mirroring of installers is allowed.

### 6. FileHorse

Submit at https://www.filehorse.com/submit/. Contact form with name, version, website, direct installer link, licence, 256x256 icon (optional), screenshots (optional) and a PAD link (optional). Windows desktop only.

The PAD link is optional here; the form matters more.

Category to pick: **System Tuning / File Management**.

> No web apps, no macOS-only or mobile-only products. VirusTotal and Safe Browsing scans decide; PUP = delisting.

### 7. Apponic

Submit at https://www.apponic.com/submit/. PAD submission form. Their server refuses scripted GET requests, so open the page in a browser.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

> Bot-protected: curl and headless fetches get 405. Submit from a normal browser session.

### 8. SixFiles

Submit at https://www.sixfiles.com/submit. PAD submission form; also mirrored into the sixfiles network.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

> Bot-protected against curl (403). Use a browser.

### 9. RocketDownload

Submit at https://www.rocketdownload.com/. PAD URL accepted; the submit page sits behind the developer login.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.
 Create the developer account first.

> Free account required before you can see the PAD field.

### 10. EuroDownload

Submit at https://eurodownload.com/contact-us/. No self-serve form: send the PAD URL through the contact form and ask for a listing.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

> Editorial site; the PAD is the fastest way to give them every field at once.

### 11. SoftSea

Submit at https://www.softsea.com/contact.html. Email-only submission. Send the PAD URL and the product's home page.

The PAD URL is accepted and is the fastest route.

> Small catalogue, slow turnaround. Worth one email per release, no more.

### 12. DownloadFrenzy

Submit at https://www.downloadfrenzy.com/addpad.aspx. AddPAD form: PAD URL plus their category.

The PAD file is the only accepted format — nothing else gets in.

> Bot-protected against curl (403); use a browser. Low editorial effort on their side.

### 13. MajorGeeks

Submit at https://www.majorgeeks.com/content/page/submit_software.html. Editorial, founded 2001: their editors research, scan and test everything. Free, nofollow. Manual form, no PAD.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.

Category to pick: **System Tools**.

> Being first to a category matters more than volume here; a good screenshot and a short feature list get you in.

### 14. SnapFiles

Submit at https://www.snapfiles.com/dev/. Developer Center with its own form. New accounts are temporarily closed: they ask you to email TempSubmissions@snapfiles.com to propose software.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.
 Create the developer account first.

> Email first, ask whether they are taking new listings, then submit. Do not expect a PAD import.

### 15. FileCroco

Submit at https://www.filecroco.com/submit/. Explicitly no automated submissions: 'we do not take any automated submissions (PAD files or else)'. Contact form only, then they test the product.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.

> Send a personal message, not a template. Quality bar is usability, not features.

### 16. Softonic

Submit at https://publishers.softonic.com/. Publishing Center: create or claim the listing yourself, upload the binary, manage versions.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.

Category to pick: **Utilities & Tools**.
 Create the developer account first.

> Largest reach of the twenty. Their scanner is strict about bundled installers.

### 17. MacUpdate

Submit at https://www.macupdate.com/content/submit. Submit an app through the site: name, version, download, icon, screenshots, description.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.
 Create the developer account first.

> macOS only. Use it for the macOS archive of the same release, not the Windows one.

### 18. SourceForge

Submit at https://sourceforge.net/create/. Project hosting and a software listing; files are mirrored for free. No PAD import.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.
 Create the developer account first.

> Only worth it if you are willing to keep a project page current; it is not a directory drop.

### 19. AlternativeTo

Submit at https://alternativeto.net/submit/. Community catalogue: submit the app, then link it as an alternative to the product it replaces.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.

Category to pick: **Productivity / File Management**.
 Create the developer account first.

> No files hosted: they link to your site. Best discovery channel of the non-PAD group.

### 20. Software Informer

Submit at https://software.informer.com/. Catalogue tied to the UpdateStar client; listings are added by their editors from vendor requests.

This one ignores PAD files: fill their form.
 Create the developer account first.

> Contact them through the site and point at the PAD URL; the listing is then maintained from your releases.

## Before you press submit

1. `pad.xml` is uploaded to the URL above and opens in a browser.
2. The icon and screenshot URLs in it open, over HTTPS, at the exact sizes they promise.
3. The download links return the current archives.
4. `tools/check_pad.py` passes for this product.
