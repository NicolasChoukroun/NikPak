Questions

How do I receive my key?
By e-mail, right after the payment (crypto: after confirmation on the blockchain). Open NikPak, click Register and enter the name, the e-mail address and the key.
Can I try NikPak before buying?
Yes. The evaluation copy has no restriction. Individuals may keep using it free of charge for personal use; organisations have 40 days.
What counts as a seat?
One computer on which NikPak is installed or one user who uses it, whichever count is higher. Every seat of an order gets the per-seat price of its volume tier.
Do I pay for updates?
No. A licence is valid for the version purchased and, unless stated otherwise at purchase, for all later 1.x versions.
Do I need a licence for unNik?
No. unNik is freeware for everyone, companies included.
I lost my key.
Write to us from the e-mail address used for the purchase, with NikPak in the subject. The key is only ever sent to that address.
Can I get an invoice?
Paddle issues invoices with your company details and handles the taxes of your country. For the other methods, ask us through the contact form.
